Wikollector

Collect, trade and battle cards made from Wikipedia articles.

Support

Something not working, a purchase that didn't arrive, or a question? Email wikollector.support@gmail.com and include your player handle if you have one.

  • Restoring purchases: open the Gem shop and tap Restore Purchases.
  • Moving to a new phone: sign in with Apple on both phones, and your save follows you.
  • Deleting your account: Profile → Delete account.

Privacy policy

Card text and images come from Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons under their own licences. Wikollector isn't affiliated with the Wikimedia Foundation.
